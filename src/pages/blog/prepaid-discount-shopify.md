If Cash on Delivery makes up a large share of your orders, you know the cost: refused parcels, return shipping, and cash that takes weeks to reach your account. The most effective fix isn't to remove COD, which many customers still rely on. It's to give them a good reason to pay upfront instead.

A small discount for prepaid orders does exactly that. Here is how to set one up on Shopify, where discounts normally can't depend on the payment method a customer chooses.

## Why a prepaid discount works

Most shoppers who choose COD aren't strongly attached to it. They pick it out of habit, or because nothing tells them a card is better. A visible saving changes that:

- **More prepaid orders** means fewer refused deliveries and return trips.
- **Faster cash flow,** because you're paid at checkout, not after the courier settles.
- **Better customers,** because someone who pays upfront is committed to receiving the order.

The discount can be modest. A few percent, or a small fixed amount, is often enough to change the default choice, and it usually costs you less than one refused COD parcel.

[ChargeIt](/products/chargeit) applies a fee or a discount based on the payment method the customer picks.

1. **Install ChargeIt** (free) and open it from your Shopify admin.
2. **Turn on the payment method selector** for your cart page, so shoppers choose how they'll pay before checkout.
3. **Create a rule,** or start from a template.
4. **Choose your prepaid methods,** for example card payments.
5. **Set the discount:** a percentage or a fixed amount, flat or tiered by cart subtotal.
6. **Name the discount line,** for example "Prepaid discount", so customers see what they've saved.
7. **Save.**

When a shopper picks a prepaid method in the cart, the discount appears right away and the total drops. Checkout then offers only the method they chose, so nobody can take the discount and switch to COD at the last step.

## Tell customers about it

A discount only changes behaviour if people know about it before they reach the cart. ChargeIt includes an **announcement bar** for the top of your store, with an optional countdown. Use it for a message like "Save 5% when you pay by card", or run it as a limited-time offer.

## Choosing the right amount

Work it out from your own numbers. Compare what an average refused COD order costs you, including return shipping and any lost stock, with the discount you'd give on a prepaid order. If the discount costs less than the losses it prevents, it pays for itself. Start small, watch how many customers switch, and adjust.

You can also:

- **Tier the discount** by cart subtotal, giving a bigger saving on larger orders, where a refused COD parcel hurts most
- **Limit it by country,** if COD is only a problem in some markets
- **Combine it with a COD fee,** so the gap between paying upfront and paying on delivery is easy to see. See our guide on [adding a Cash on Delivery fee](/blog/add-cash-on-delivery-fee-shopify).
