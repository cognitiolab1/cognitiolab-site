If your Shopify checkout shows a payment option with a generic name, something like "Online Payment Options," many of your customers won't recognize it. They know brands: JazzCash, Easypaisa, their bank's card. When the label at checkout doesn't match what they expect to pay with, they hesitate, and hesitation at checkout costs you sales.

The good news: you can rename any payment method to a clear, trusted label in just a couple of minutes, with no code and no theme editing.

## Why payment gateway labels are often confusing

Most Pakistani and regional payment gateways bundle several methods (cards, JazzCash, Easypaisa) behind a single checkout entry with a generic name. That name usually reflects the gateway provider, not the payment method your customer actually uses. So a shopper who wants to pay with JazzCash sees an unfamiliar label and isn't sure they're in the right place.

## How to rename a payment method with PayShift

[PayShift](https://apps.shopify.com/payshift) lets you rename payment methods at checkout with a simple rule:

1. **Install PayShift** (free) and open it from your Shopify admin.
2. **Create a new rule** and choose **Rename**.
3. **Select the payment method** with the confusing label, and enter the clear name you want customers to see. For example, change "Online Payment Options" to "JazzCash."
4. **Save.** The new label appears at checkout instantly.

## Other things you can do

Beyond renaming, PayShift can also **hide** payment methods based on conditions (like hiding Cash on Delivery for high-value orders) and **reorder** methods to put your preferred option first. It works the same way for shipping methods, too.
