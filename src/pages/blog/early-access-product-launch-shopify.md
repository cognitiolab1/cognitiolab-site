Early access is one of the simplest ways to make your best customers feel valued. Your newsletter subscribers or repeat buyers get first pick of a new collection, a day or two before everyone else. It rewards loyalty, builds anticipation, and often sells out the most popular sizes before the public launch even starts.

The tricky part on Shopify is keeping the products hidden from everyone else. If you publish the products, everyone can buy them. If you don't, your VIPs can't either. Here is how to have both.

## The idea

You publish the new products as normal, then put a rule in front of them:

- **Everyone else** sees a coming-soon placeholder instead of the product, so the launch is visible but not yet buyable.
- **Your VIPs** get in through a key you give them, and see the full product with its price and Add to Cart button.

When the launch goes public, you switch the rule off. No re-publishing, no copying products.

## Choosing the key

There are three good ways to let VIPs in. Pick the one that fits how you reach them:

- **A secret link.** Send a link in your VIP email. Anyone who opens it gets access. It's the easiest option for shoppers, but anyone they forward it to can use it too.
- **A passcode.** Share a code such as `EARLY26`. Shoppers type it to get in. It feels exclusive, and you can change it any time.
- **A customer tag.** Tag your VIP customers in Shopify, for example `vip`. Only those customers, when logged in, get access. It's the tightest option, because the access is tied to their account.

## Launch day

When it's time to open the launch to everyone, turn the rule off in GateKeeper. The products become available to all shoppers immediately.

A few tips for a smooth early-access launch:

- **Tell VIPs how long their head start lasts**, for example "48 hours before everyone else". A deadline gets people to act.
- **Use a customer tag for your most exclusive drops**, and a secret link for broader "subscribers first" launches.
- **Test the rule** by opening your store in a private window, where you're not logged in, to see what the public sees.
